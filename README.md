# Bridgerton Digital Party

A refined invitation experience using separate pages for each step.

## Folder structure

- `index.html` — redirects to the newspaper landing page
- `newspaper/index.html` — Lady Whistledown society article with portrait and stamp
- `envelope/index.html` — envelope reveal scene with letter and audio
- `guestbook/index.html` — digital guestbook for messages
- `cake/index.html` — celebratory cake page
- `closing/index.html` — elegant curtain closing finale with audio
- `styles.css` — shared styling, palette, and page animations
- `script.js` — shared page logic, navigation, audio, and guestbook storage
- `assets/` — your images and audio files

## Assets to add

Copy your own files into `assets/`:

- `assets/bridgerton-music.mp3` — background violin or classical audio loop
- `assets/mandisa-voice.mp3` — your voice greeting
- `assets/audio/closing.mp4` — closing curtain audio finale
- `assets/assets/mandisa-photo.jpeg` — portrait for the Whistledown landing page (AI-generated)
- `assets/assets/ladywhitsledown.jpeg` — Lady Whistledown seal stamp

## How to use

1. Open `index.html` in your browser.
2. The page redirects to the Whistledown newspaper article with your AI portrait and Lady Whistledown stamp.
3. Click **Reveal the envelope** to open the invitation in-page.
4. Listen for the music and letter reveal.
5. Click **Proceed to the celebration** to open the guestbook.
6. Fill in your name and message.
7. After submission, you proceed to the cake page with **Care for a Slice?**.
8. Click **Bid Adieu** for the grand finale: elegant red curtains closing with your closing audio.
